Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Classroom Priorities ("the app", "we") is a free homework planner at classroommanager.site. This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it. The short version: the app has no server. Your information goes directly from Google to your own browser, is stored only on your device, and is never sent to us, sold, or used for advertising.

Information the app accesses

When you choose "Sign in with Google", Google asks you to approve the permissions below. The app only asks for the last two when you use those features.

PermissionWhat the app does with it
See your Google Classroom classes
classroom.courses.readonly		Lists your active classes so their names, colors and assignments can be shown.
See your Classroom coursework and grades
classroom.coursework.me.readonly		Reads your assignments, due dates, points and your own submission status (for example turned in or missing) to rank and plan your work, and shows returned grades next to finished work.
See your calendars
calendar.readonly		Lists your Google Calendars so you can choose which to show. Events from calendars you turn on appear in the calendar view and the study plan avoids them.
Your name, email and profile picture
userinfo.profile, userinfo.email		Shows your picture and name on the profile button and your email in the profile menu.
See your Google Tasks (optional)
tasks.readonly		Only if you click "Import from Google Tasks": lists your unfinished tasks so you can choose which to add as assignments.
Make calendars the app creates (optional)
calendar.app.created		Only if you click "Sync to Google Calendar": creates a calendar named "Study Plan" and adds, updates or removes your study sessions on it. It can't see or change your other calendars this way.

The app never turns in, edits or deletes your schoolwork, never changes your existing calendars, and never posts anything for you.

Where your information is stored

  • On your device only. Assignments, calendar events, your own assignments, study plan, settings, checklists and study history are saved in your browser's local storage so the app opens quickly and works offline.
  • Sign-in. The temporary access token from Google is kept only for your current browser tab (about an hour) and is never stored on a server.
  • Backups. If you use "Download backup", a file is saved to your device. It's yours; the app doesn't upload it anywhere.
  • Photo scan. If you use "Scan a photo", the image is read on your device and isn't uploaded.

Sharing

We don't have a copy of your information, so we don't sell, rent or share it with anyone. Information received from Google is used only to provide the app's features to you.

Classroom Priorities' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information from Google APIs is not used for advertising, is not used to train AI models, and is not read by people.

Services the app loads

Like most websites, loading the app connects your browser to a few services. Each can see your IP address and basic browser information under its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website (GitHub privacy statement).
  • Google provides sign-in, the Classroom, Calendar and Tasks APIs, and the Plus Jakarta Sans font (Google privacy policy).
  • jsDelivr serves the text-recognition library, only when you use photo scan (jsDelivr privacy policy).

The app doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies, or analytics.

Your choices

  • Remove saved data: open the profile menu and choose Sign out, which clears the saved Classroom data and sign-in from your browser. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes everything else (your own assignments, plan and settings).
  • Remove the app's access to your Google account: go to myaccount.google.com/connections and remove Classroom Priorities.
  • "Study Plan" calendar: you can delete it any time in Google Calendar.

Students and schools

Classroom Priorities is made for students. It doesn't collect personal information on any server, so we don't hold student records. Students under 13 should use the app only with a parent's permission or through a school account their school has approved. Schools using Google Workspace for Education can control whether students may use the app in the Google Admin console.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
