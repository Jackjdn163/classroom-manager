Your homework, sorted.

Classroom Priorities is a free planner for students. It shows all of your Google Classroom assignments in one ranked list, plans when to do them around school and your calendar, and helps you focus.

Open Classroom Priorities Privacy policy

What it does

Every assignment, rankedMissing work, what's due in the next 48 hours, this week and later, with links straight to each assignment in Google Classroom.
A study plan for the weekSpreads your work across the days before each due date, around school, practices and your Google Calendar events.
Calendar with your classesA month view and an hour-by-hour week view with your class schedule, study sessions and due times.
Focus timer and streaksA full-screen timer for study sessions, a daily streak and how much you studied this week.
Your own to-dos tooAdd your own assignments, import Google Tasks, and optionally copy your study sessions into a Google Calendar.
Private by designThere's no Classroom Priorities server. Your data goes straight from Google to your browser and stays there.

How it uses your Google account

When you sign in with Google, Classroom Priorities asks for permission to read your Google Classroom classes and your own assignments and submissions, read your Google Calendars, and see your name, email and profile picture. Only if you use those features does it ask to read your Google Tasks, or to create a "Study Plan" calendar and add study sessions to it. It never turns in, edits or deletes your schoolwork, and it can't change your existing calendars.

All of this happens in your browser. Nothing is sent to us or sold, and nothing is used for advertising. See the privacy policy for details.

Classroom Priorities' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Using a school account?

Some schools only allow apps their IT team has approved. If you see "Access blocked" when signing in, ask your school's Google Workspace admin to allow Classroom Priorities.
